### Objective
Inspect a raw phishing `.eml` sample in a safe text editor, extract and analyze the `Received` hops, verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC headers, and use CyberChef to safely defang malicious URLs (e.g., transforming `http://bad.com` into `hxxp[://]bad[.]com`).
